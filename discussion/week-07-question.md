title: "LDA training error vs cross validation error"
author: "Eric Chen (ec-03)"

---

In LDA, training error can look much better than cross-validated error, and the shared-covariance assumption may not hold across classes. How would you use validation error and a comparison of class covariance matrices to decide whether the problem is overfitting, a violated assumption, or both? Which remedy, such as QDA, regularized discriminant analysis, or dropping predictors, would each diagnosis point to?
