How can AI agents check the legitimacy of model fit? For example, some modeling techniques may seem to work well on immediate validation metrics while being poorly suited to the application domain. How can practitioners distinguish a genuinely appropriate model from one that only appears convincing under weak evaluation? What diagnostics or safeguards should be used when a method like cross-validation on financial data may still produce misleading conclusions?
